About EFAI

Creating a cleaner, greener and more sustainable Africa.

Who We Are

Enviro Friend Africa Initiative (EFAI) is a Rwandan environmental initiative focused on transforming recyclable waste into opportunities for environmental conservation and community empowerment.

We work with households, schools, businesses and communities to promote responsible waste management, recycling, tree planting and environmental education.

Our Mission

To transform waste into environmental, social and economic opportunities while empowering communities to protect and restore nature.

Our Vision

A cleaner, greener and more sustainable Africa where waste becomes a valuable resource for community development.

Our Core Values

  • 🌱 Environmental Responsibility
  • 🤝 Community Empowerment
  • ♻️ Innovation Through Recycling
  • 🌍 Sustainable Development
  • 👥 Youth Leadership
