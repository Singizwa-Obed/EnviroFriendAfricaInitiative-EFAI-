Founder's Story

From personal struggle to community solution

My Journey

Enviro Friend Africa Initiative (EFAI) was founded by Singizwa Obed, a young Rwandan environmental leader whose vision was shaped by the challenges he experienced growing up in a rural community.

In the village where I was born, families faced challenges with essential services. Waste collection costs and water bills created a difficult situation for many households, including my own family.

Because of these challenges, my family had to make difficult decisions. Instead of using piped water, we collected water from a distant source. The long journey to access water shaped my understanding of the struggles many communities face.

Later, when I moved to Kigali in search of better opportunities, I saw another challenge: large amounts of recyclable waste were being thrown away while many communities continued facing economic difficulties.

I realized that these two problems were connected. Waste was not just rubbish — it was a resource that could create opportunities and support communities.

This realization inspired me to create Enviro Friend Africa Initiative (EFAI), an initiative focused on transforming waste into opportunity.

Our Waste-to-Water Inspiration

Through research in Kigali, we discovered that recyclable materials collected from households can generate value that helps support essential needs. This inspired our Waste-to-Water Program, where participating households contribute recyclable materials and receive support according to the value of collected materials.

Our Dream

Our dream is to build a cleaner, greener Africa where environmental protection creates opportunities for communities, especially young people.

"Transforming waste into opportunity for a cleaner, greener, and more sustainable Africa."
