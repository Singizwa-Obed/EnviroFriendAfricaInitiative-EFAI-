About EFAI

Enviro Friend Africa Initiative (EFAI) is an environmental conservation initiative dedicated to creating sustainable solutions through recycling, tree planting, environmental education, and community empowerment.

We believe waste is not useless. Plastic straps, metals, papers, glass, and unused electronics can become valuable resources that support communities while protecting our environment.

Our Projects

♻️ Plastic Recycling

Collecting plastic straps and recyclable materials and transforming waste into environmental and economic opportunities.

💧 Water Support Partnership

We partner with homes by collecting recyclable straps and supporting part of their water bills through recycling activities.

🌳 Tree Planting

Mobilizing communities and young people to plant trees and restore nature.

🧹 Clean Africa Campaign

Organizing environmental cleanups to create healthier communities.
