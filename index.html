About EFAI

Creating environmental solutions through community action

Who We Are

Enviro Friend Africa Initiative (EFAI) is a Rwandan environmental initiative dedicated to transforming recyclable waste into opportunities for environmental protection, community empowerment, and sustainable development.

We work with households, schools, businesses, and communities to collect recyclable materials, promote responsible waste management, plant trees, and educate young people about environmental conservation.

Our Mission

To transform waste into environmental, social, and economic opportunities while empowering communities to protect and restore nature.

Our Vision

A cleaner, greener, and more sustainable Africa where waste becomes a valuable resource for community development.

Founder's Story

From personal struggle to community solution

Enviro Friend Africa Initiative (EFAI) was founded by Singizwa Obed, a young Rwandan environmental leader whose vision was shaped by the challenges he experienced growing up in a rural community.

In the village where I was born, many families struggled with the cost of essential services. Waste collection expenses and water bills created a difficult situation for households, including my own family.

This experience helped me understand the connection between environmental challenges and community needs. Later, after moving to Kigali, I saw valuable recyclable materials being thrown away while many families continued to face economic challenges.

I realized that waste was not simply rubbish. It was a resource that could create opportunities, protect the environment, and support communities.

This realization inspired the creation of Enviro Friend Africa Initiative (EFAI).

Waste-to-Water Program

Connecting environmental protection with community support

EFAI believes that recyclable waste has value beyond disposal. Through our Waste-to-Water Program, households participate in collecting recyclable materials while contributing to environmental conservation.

The collected materials are sorted and prepared for recycling. The value generated from these materials helps support participating households by contributing toward their water expenses according to the value of the recyclables collected.
