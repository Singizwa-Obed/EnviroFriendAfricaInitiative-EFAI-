Our Programs

Creating environmental solutions through community action

♻️ Plastic Recycling

We collect plastic waste and promote recycling solutions that reduce pollution and protect our environment.

💧 Waste-to-Water Program

We connect recycling with community support by helping participating households benefit from the value of recyclable materials they provide.

🌳 Tree Planting

We encourage tree planting activities to restore nature and fight environmental degradation.

🧹 Community Clean-up

We organize clean-up activities that create healthier and cleaner communities.

📚 Environmental Education

We educate communities and young people about recycling, conservation, and sustainable living.

👥 Youth Empowerment

We inspire young people to become environmental leaders and create solutions for a sustainable future.
