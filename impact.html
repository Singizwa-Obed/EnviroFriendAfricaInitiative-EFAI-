Our Impact

Creating positive environmental and community change through action.

♻️ Environmental Impact

EFAI reduces environmental pollution by collecting recyclable materials and promoting responsible waste management.

💧 Community Impact

Through our Waste-to-Water Program, recyclable materials become a resource that can support households with essential needs.

🌳 Climate Impact

We support tree planting and environmental conservation to protect nature and contribute to a greener Africa.

👥 Youth Impact

We empower young people to become environmental leaders and create sustainable solutions in their communities.
